Consumer requirements and remaining limits:

- Before `wcd_irq_exit`, callers must stop and drain upstream dispatch through the returned domain and free all nested IRQ handlers. Keep `irq_info`, codec name and device alive through that teardown; callers must not use the returned domain after exit. The original WCD9378 consumer publishes `virq` in `tx_swr_dev->slave_irq`; its upcoming port must clear/drain this borrowed pointer before exit. Its component-registration error path must also free all three requested watchdog IRQs before exit. No WCD9378 consumer has been imported or modified by this group.
- Regulator helpers retain the native requirement for caller serialization and balanced enables/disables; a boolean vote is not a multi-user reference count. Hardware rollback can itself fail; the primary operation error remains returned. Multi-pin wake and multi-supply cleanup remain sequential operations that can partly complete before an error.
- A successful `msm_cdc_init_wcd_supply` owns one device reference, which its caller must eventually release with `put_device(cdc_supply->dev)` after the supply is unused. The caller must keep the ASoC component registered and synchronize its use/removal; a device reference alone does not retain driver resources. No calls to these two WCD-supply helpers were found in the native audio tree beyond their definitions during this scope.
- No actual Linux module load, driver binding/removal, IRQ dispatch, regulator voltage, MMIO, LPASS/codec operation, DSP/audio playback, or phone boot was tested. The next native chain consists of LPASS CDC/BT parents before the dependent codecs and machine driver.
